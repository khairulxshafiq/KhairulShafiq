About

Mohammad Khairul Shafiq bin Mohd Nizam — Khairul Shafiq — is based in Shah Alam, Malaysia. His practice is technology solutioning: he works as a solution lead, technology advisor, and consultant.

He has spent more than ten years in IT. He is a Microsoft 365 Global Admin and a subject-matter expert in Microsoft 365, Intune and endpoint management, and Entra ID identity.

The direction of the practice is infra-to-AI solution architecture: retrieval-augmented generation, local and private AI, and agents. He shares that work in public. He founded TanAir and leads the product.
