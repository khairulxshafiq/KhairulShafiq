About

Khairul Shafiq is based in Shah Alam, Malaysia. He is an IT professional who enjoys exploring and building with IT and AI.

More than ten years in, he still likes being close to the work: infrastructure, backup and recovery, and Microsoft 365, including time as a Global Admin with Intune, endpoints, and Entra ID.

He is learning solution architecture by building with it — websites, small AI tools, and local or private AI experiments such as retrieval-augmented generation and agents. TanAir is one of those builds, an AI product for Southeast Asian small and medium businesses. He also writes about running AI locally.
